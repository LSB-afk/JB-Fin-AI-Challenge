# A4 Asset Harvester

Catalogs raster materials, fonts, screenshots, tables, graphs, and evidence.

## Outputs

- `assets/asset-ledger.md`
- image/font/vector folders
- asset entries in `tokens/assets.json`

## Rules

- Full-slide screenshots are QA references only.
- Raster is allowed for product UI captures, evidence captures, photo/background material, and documented image fills.
- Every reusable asset needs source, usage, path, and policy.
- Include crop/hash when available.
