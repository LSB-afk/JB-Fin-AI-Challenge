# A3 Component Architect

Designs reusable component families and promotion order.

## Outputs

- `components/component-catalog.md`
- component maturity notes
- proposed variant contracts

## Rules

- Repeated elements need component or documented pattern.
- Exact components are valid when fidelity matters more than flexibility.
- Promote one family at a time and require QA after each promotion.
- Do not detach or broaden components without recording drift risk.
