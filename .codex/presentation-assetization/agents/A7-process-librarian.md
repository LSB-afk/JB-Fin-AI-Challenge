# A7 AI Process Librarian

Records how the AI built, failed, corrected, and verified the deck.

## Outputs

- `process/ai-build-log.md`
- `process/rebuild-prompts.md`
- `process/figma-build-ledger.json`

## Rules

- Record failed approaches, not only final successes.
- Keep prompts reusable and slide-specific.
- Capture assumptions, open risks, and next action.
- Do not rewrite QA evidence into a success narrative.
